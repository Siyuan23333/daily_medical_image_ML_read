A Multi-Structural Radiomic Digital Phenotyping Framework for Deciphering Preclinical Evolution of Degenerative Meniscal Tears.

Bone and skeletalscore 9 Advanced Science · IF≈9.72026-10-30S Li, H Liu, Y Zhu et al.

Conventional MRI often misses the subvisual tissue changes that precede macroscopic degenerative meniscal tears (DMTs), which are central to knee osteoarthritis progression. The study used 3D double-echo steady-state MRI from a longitudinal cohort of 674 participants, though the abstract does not specify the number of centres or data source. The authors built a multi-structural digital risk stratification framework (MSC-RM) that combines standardized radiomic signatures from six knee structures (femur, tibia, cartilages, and menisci) with baseline clinical risk profiles. It achieved 4-year predictive performance with an AUC of 0.871, and survival analysis showed high-risk individuals had a 6.12-fold greater hazard of developing DMTs than the low-risk group; a reader test with five resident surgeons showed Kappa rising from 0.38 to 0.55. The work is notable for shifting from reactive lesion detection to proactive risk stratification and for showing that radiomic digital biomarkers can improve diagnostic consistency among less experienced readers.

CT-based multimodal fusion model for preoperative clear cell renal cell carcinoma grading: A multicenter study.

Radiomicsscore 9 European Journal of Radiology · IF≈3.72026-10-24Y Wu, H Wang, C Liu et al.

The study addresses preoperative WHO/ISUP grading of clear cell renal cell carcinoma (low vs high grade) from contrast-enhanced CT, a task relevant to non-invasive risk stratification. It used a retrospective multicentre cohort of 1,052 histologically confirmed ccRCC patients from four institutions, with a primary cohort of 665 patients at Center A (465 training, 200 internal validation) and 387 patients from Centers B-D as an independent external test set, using corticomedullary phase CECT. Single-modality classifiers were built with ensemble machine learning on clinical variables, multiscale radiomic features (intratumoral, habitat, and peritumoral regions), and deep learning representations (2D, 2.5D, 3D), then combined by soft voting, with SHAP for interpretability and ROC analysis for evaluation. The weighted soft voting model (SoftVote-Opt) achieved AUCs of 0.761 (95% CI 0.675-0.839) internally and 0.745 (95% CI 0.675-0.813) externally, with MCCs of 0.347 and 0.302, and SHAP identified peritumoral 5 mm radiomic features as the largest contributors, followed by intratumoral radiomics, with deep learning and clinical features contributing less. The authors describe the performance as moderate and the work as exploratory rather than evidence of clinical superiority, with the main contribution being an evaluation of multimodal fusion combining clinical, deep learning, and multiscale radiomic information for ccRCC grading.

Deep learning predicts IDH1 mutation status in acute myeloid leukemia from bone marrow smears.

Medical imaging MLscore 9 NPJ Precision Oncology · IF≈9.22026-10-29JN Eckardt, CBV Reddy, A Srisuwananukorn et al.

Reliable identification of IDH1 mutation status in acute myeloid leukemia is needed because IDH1-mutant cases are eligible for targeted therapy. The study used bone marrow smear whole-slide images from 436 AML patients across four centers in Germany and the US. An attention-based multiple-instance learning model was trained to predict IDH1 mutation status, achieving a mean single pooled ROC-AUC of 0.81. In top tile analysis, model predictions for IDH1 were associated with immature leukemia phenotypes. The work indicates that mutation status can be inferred from routine bone marrow smear images, linking model predictions to interpretable morphological features.

This study asks whether IDH1 mutation status in AML, a recurrent alteration (~8% of patients) with FDA-approved targeted inhibitors (ivosidenib, olutasidenib), can be inferred from routine May-Gruenwald-Giemsa bone marrow smear whole slide images. The authors assembled a four-center retrospective cohort of 525 WSIs from 436 AML patients (Dresden, OSU, MLL, LMU), deliberately enriched for IDH1-mutated cases to roughly 1:1. A customized U-Net segmented diagnostic ROIs; a Faster R-CNN, trained on 387 manually curated tiles, selected high-quality tiles (top 40 per patient); a weakly supervised ViT-Base (patch 32) served as a tile-level feature extractor; and an attention-based multiple-instance learning head aggregated 768-d CLS embeddings into slide-level predictions, averaged to patient level. Three-fold patient-level cross-validation yielded macro ROC-AUC 0.806 (95% CI 0.744-0.862), accuracy 0.741, F1 0.725, improving on mean-pooled tile classification (F1 0.677, accuracy 0.710). The genuine novelty is a new digital biomarker candidate for IDH1 in cytomorphology, plus the demonstration that informative morphology is sparse and requires attention-based aggregation. The effect size is modest, the cohort is enriched and retrospective, and explainability analyses did not localize a specific cellular phenotype, so this is proof-of-concept rather than a deployable assay.

Predictive and risk stratification performance of CCTA-derived pericoronary adipose tissue radiomics for major adverse cardiovascular events: retrospective development and prospective validation.

Radiomicsscore 9 La Radiologia Medica · IF≈4.62026-10-30H Qi, Y Wu, X Zhang et al.

The study addresses whether radiomics of pericoronary adipose tissue (PCAT) from coronary CT angiography can predict major adverse cardiovascular events (MACEs) and stratify patient risk better than fat attenuation index (FAI) or clinical models. Development used a retrospective single-centre cohort of patients with suspected coronary artery disease who underwent CCTA between June 2017 and June 2020 with 5-year MACE follow-up, and prospective validation used 578 patients with suspected CAD undergoing CCTA, stratified into high- and low-risk groups by the optimal PCAT radiomics cutoff. Machine learning was used to build and compare PCAT radiomics, FAI, and clinical models; 14 PCAT features (5 first-order, 9 texture) were selected. The PCAT radiomics model significantly outperformed the FAI and clinical models for MACE prediction (P < 0.001), and only it showed significantly higher MACE risk in the high-risk versus low-risk group (HR=2.611, 95% CI 1.806-3.775, P < 0.001), with reduced MACE-free survival in the high-risk group (P < 0.001), while FAI and clinical models showed no significant between-group differences (all P > 0.05). The work indicates that CCTA-derived PCAT radiomics, driven mainly by worsening stable or unstable angina, may offer better MACE risk stratification than FAI or clinical factors alone.

This single-center study asks whether radiomics of pericoronary adipose tissue (PCAT) from CCTA predicts major adverse cardiovascular events (MACEs) better than the fat attenuation index (FAI) or a clinical model, and whether such a model can stratify risk prospectively. In a retrospective development phase, 200 MACE cases (5-year follow-up) were 1:1 matched to 200 event-free controls; 5064 PCAT features (first-order, texture, shape) were extracted from proximal LAD, LCX and RCA segments using an AI tool, reduced by Pearson correlation filtering and LASSO to 14 features (5 first-order, 9 texture), and modeled with several classifiers, with stochastic gradient descent selected. Diagnostic accuracy of MRI-derived imaging biomarkers and machine learning-based classification in distal and myofibrillar myopathies

Medical imaging MLscore 8 European Radiology · IF 4.7journal list2026-10-30Bortolani, Sara, Monforte, Mauro, Verdu-Diaz, Jose et al.

The study addresses the difficulty of distinguishing genetically heterogeneous distal and myofibrillar myopathies, where disease-specific muscle imaging signatures could aid diagnosis. It retrospectively analysed muscle MRI and CT scans from a multicentre national cohort of 165 patients (mean age 48 ± 16 years; 87 men), including 138 molecularly confirmed cases spanning 19 disease genes, with 6350 muscles systematically evaluated. The authors characterised disease-specific patterns of muscle involvement (e.g., early biceps femoris short head involvement in GNE myopathy, fat pockets in myotilinopathy, early flexor hallucis longus involvement in desminopathy), calculated their sensitivity and specificity, and trained supervised machine learning models on muscle scores and imaging-derived features, comparing them by balanced accuracy. Imaging patterns showed high sensitivity and specificity (75 to 100%), and XGBoost performed best, distinguishing among 11 genetic disease entities with a balanced accuracy of 69%. The work provides reproducible imaging biomarkers and a machine learning framework intended to prioritise likely genetic diagnoses and guide targeted genetic testing in these heterogeneous inherited muscle disorders.

AnySleep: a channel-agnostic deep learning system for high-resolution sleep staging in multi-center cohorts

Medical imaging MLscore 7 npj Digital Medicine · IF 15.1journal list2026-10-30Grieger, Niklas, Raskob, Jannik, Mehrkanoon, Siamak et al.

Manual sleep staging of polysomnography is labor-intensive, and traditional 30-s epoch scoring varies across centres in electrode count and montage, hindering harmonized multi-centre studies and short-timescale biomarker discovery. The authors trained and validated a deep neural network on over 20,000 overnight recordings (more than 200,000 h of EEG and EOG) from 28 datasets across multiple clinics. The channel-agnostic model scores sleep from any EEG or EOG data at adjustable temporal resolutions, and performance improves with more channels while remaining strong with only EOG or single frontal, central, or occipital EEG derivations. It surpasses or equals established baselines at 30-s epochs, and at sub-30-s timescales it captures short wake intrusions consistent with arousals and improves prediction of obstructive sleep apnea, narcolepsy type 1, and insomnia over 30-s scoring. The model is publicly released to support large-scale studies with heterogeneous electrode setups.
