Core (always relevant):
- Machine learning, deep learning, or foundation models applied to medical images: CT, MRI, X-ray, DXA, ultrasound, PET/SPECT, digital pathology, ophthalmic and dermatologic imaging. Includes segmentation, detection, classification, reconstruction, registration, generation, report generation, and vision-language models for medical images.
- Radiomics in any organ system or modality: feature extraction, harmonization, reproducibility, radiomics-based prediction or prognosis models, delta-radiomics, habitat analysis.
- Bone and skeletal topics with any computational or quantitative-imaging method: osteoporosis, fracture risk and fracture detection, bone mineral density, DXA, HR-pQCT, opportunistic CT screening, bone age, skeletal segmentation, osteoarthritis imaging, bone tumours, orthopaedic and spine imaging, bone microarchitecture and finite element analysis.
- Bone densitometry and microarchitecture imaging is a special interest: studies in which HR-pQCT, pQCT, DXA or the trabecular bone score is the central measurement or method count as core even without machine learning. This covers validation, reproducibility and reference data, microarchitecture and finite-element analyses, method comparisons, opportunistic or automated densitometry, and clinical studies whose main results are HR-pQCT or DXA measurements. Papers that merely report BMD as one of many outcomes do not qualify.

Included (relevant when the paper is clearly about ML applied to clinical data):
- EHR-based prediction, risk scoring, and phenotyping.
- Clinical NLP, clinical large language model evaluation, and LLMs for clinical documentation or decision support.
- Multimodal models that combine imaging with clinical, text, or omics data.
- Methods papers (uncertainty, fairness, domain shift, federated learning, foundation-model adaptation) when evaluated on medical data.

Excluded:
- Drug discovery, protein structure, molecular, cell-level, or genomics-only ML with no clinical data component.
- Pure ML methods papers with no medical data.
- Non-ML clinical papers: trials, epidemiology, mechanistic biology without computational methods. This applies to the bone journals too, except for the HR-pQCT and DXA studies described under Core.
- Editorials, errata, corrections, news, commentaries.
- Reviews and surveys are kept but must be flagged as reviews.
